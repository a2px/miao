Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026. Short version: we don't collect personal information through this website, and your location never leaves your device.

Your location

If you choose to share your location, your browser hands it to this page so it can work out how far you are from the cafe. That calculation happens entirely on your device. We never send your location to our servers or anyone else, and we don't store it. If you open directions in Google Maps or Apple Maps, your starting point is passed to that service so it can plan the route, under its own privacy policy.

You can turn location access off at any time in your browser's site settings.

Cookies and storage

We don't use cookies, analytics or ad trackers. The site saves two small settings in your browser: whether you paused animations, and whether you already watched the intro this visit. Neither identifies you.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • OpenStreetMap supplies the map. Your browser loads map images from OpenStreetMap, which receives your IP address.
  • Links to Instagram, TikTok, DoorDash, Uber Eats, Google Maps and Apple Maps take you to those services, which have their own policies.

Messages

If you message us, we use what you send only to reply. We don't sell or share it.

Questions

Send us a message on Instagram, or ask at the counter.
