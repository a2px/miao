    Questions people actually ask

    Is there a time limit?

    Nope. Your $15 is good all day. Settle in, leave, come back. We close at 2 AM and that’s the only clock we watch.

    Can two people share one all-you-can-drink pass?

    The pass is per person; it’s how we keep the price this low. Single drinks are available if your friend just wants a taste.

    Do you have caffeine-free options?

    Yes. Ask at the counter and we’ll point you to the bases that won’t keep you up. Though, to be fair, being up is kind of our thing.

    Can I customize sugar and ice?

    Always. Every drink is built to order, so dial the sweetness and ice exactly where you want them.
