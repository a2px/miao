Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to find us, check our hours and read our menu. This site is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

What we've done

  • Everything works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline, and a "Skip to main content" link at the top of each page.
  • The animated intro can be skipped, and it never plays if your device is set to reduce motion. A "Pause motion" button in the top bar stops every animation on the site.
  • Text meets AA colour-contrast ratios, and the layout works from small phones up to wide screens and at 400% zoom.
  • Buttons and links are at least 24 by 24 pixels, and most are larger.
  • Photos have text descriptions; decorative art is hidden from screen readers.
  • The social carousel never moves on its own and has Previous and Next buttons, so no swiping or dragging is required.
  • Distance updates are announced to screen readers when they appear.
